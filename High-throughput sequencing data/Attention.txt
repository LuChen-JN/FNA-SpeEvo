FOR Aptamers：In the aptamer sequencing results, although we have asked the sequencing company to remove the primer sequence, some sequences still have primer sequences and need to be further removed.

FOR Riboswitch：The sequencing results in this article are the results of reverse transcription of the RNA sequence, so first we need to replace T with U, then determine the sense strand according to the fixed base combination of the primer region in the riboswitch design, and finally remove the primer region.
